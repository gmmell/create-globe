About & credits

Approximate educational view. Earth, cloud, and city-light textures are reference imagery, not live weather. Sunlight follows the current time. The Gaia DR2 sky map is aligned using Greenwich sidereal time; precession, nutation, and atmospheric refraction are not modeled. Sky brightness and atmospheric glow are enhanced. Zoom is limited to the bundled image detail.

Earth textures: Solar System Scope, based on NASA data, CC BY 4.0. Milky Way: NASA/Goddard Scientific Visualization Studio; Gaia DR2, ESA/Gaia/DPAC. Rendered with Three.js (MIT). Textures are rendered with custom lighting and colour effects.
